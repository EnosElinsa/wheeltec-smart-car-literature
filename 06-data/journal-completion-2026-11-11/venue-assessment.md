# Current active-source venues

Historical stage snapshot. Later J01/J02 completion and owner exclusions are recorded in [current work](../current-work.md); use that page for present download actions.

This snapshot covers 442 active sources, excluding the 171 background/excluded catalog records. It reports venue identities, not a full-paper quality review or current JCR/CAS/CCF classifications.

The main robotics flagship journals are IEEE Transactions on Robotics, The International Journal of Robotics Research, and Science Robotics. RSS is a selective robotics conference; CoRL is a leading specialist robot-learning conference; ICRA and IROS are major broad robotics conferences. The collection contains substantial coverage of these venues.

IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters is a strong peer-reviewed robotics venue. Its 110 existing sources were retained, and zero new RA-L papers were downloaded in either expansion. Its shorter format and venue label alone do not imply inferior scientific work.

IEEE/ASME Transactions on Mechatronics is a leading mechatronics journal. Journal of Field Robotics and Autonomous Robots are established specialist robotics journals. TASE, Robotics and Autonomous Systems, transportation/control journals and the other listed venues have different scopes and roles; they should not all be described as robotics flagship journals. These distinctions do not establish that any individual paper is weak.

Nature Machine Intelligence and Annual Review of Control, Robotics, and Autonomous Systems were also searched for relevant work. Their selected papers still require official manual downloads and are not included in the active counts below.

A useful paper-level assessment still needs task/platform fit, appropriate baselines, real-robot evaluation, repeatability, numerical verification and limitations. Most new sources have only title/abstract screening plus transcription; extraction does not establish scientific quality.
