Historical stage snapshot. Later J01/J02 completion and owner exclusions are recorded in [current work](../current-work.md); use that page for present download actions.

On November 11, 2026, thirteen user-supplied PDFs completed 8 publisher-native pairs and 5 explicitly authorized Crossref citation exceptions. The original Downloads files were preserved. The H-series now has 59 registered sources from its 60 fixed selections: 54 native pairs and 5 registry-citation exceptions. H05 (SORA-Q, Science Robotics) remains unavailable. The earlier acquisition register retains its historical 46-pair totals; its source rows now point to the subsequent OXR extraction stage.

A bounded search nominated 108 journal candidates; 30 explicit papers were selected after title/abstract review and DOI deduplication against 580 baseline records. Of these, 20 official native pairs were downloaded and 10 remain unresolved in [manual-download.csv](manual-download.csv). RA-L was excluded. This is a purposive expansion with no claim of complete journal coverage.

T-RO, IJRR and Science Robotics remain the core robotics flagship-journal priorities. TMech is a leading mechatronics journal; JFR and Autonomous Robots are established specialist robotics journals; TASE covers automation and relevant multi-robot methods. Nature Machine Intelligence covers broader machine intelligence, while Annual Review of Control, Robotics, and Autonomous Systems is a review-focused venue. This qualitative description does not assert current CAS/JCR/CCF categories or certify individual papers.

Publisher access failures and unsupported adapters retain their exact diagnostics. Only verified official downloads enter the catalog. Original frozen H and J selections and all acquired source bytes are unchanged. Private plans retain hashes and rollback backups.
