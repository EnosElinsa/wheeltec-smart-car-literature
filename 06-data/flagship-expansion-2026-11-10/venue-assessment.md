# Baseline venue assessment

This assessment covers the 363 active sources before the H-series expansion, not all 534 catalog records: excluded/background records have a different role. Venue standing is a qualitative field judgment, not a current JCR quartile, CAS division, CCF category or paper-level quality verdict. Those ranking systems vary by edition and were not reverified here.

The active collection is dominated by recognized peer-reviewed venues. RA-L is a strong robotics venue; its 110 sources make the collection less balanced across venues. A compact RA-L article can still be a strong method paper. T-RO and IJRR often provide more space for system detail and extended evaluation. ICRA and IROS are major conferences, but their broad programs still contain variation in experimental depth and relevance.

Related control, transportation, perception and systems journals serve specific methods or background. Their fit must be judged from the task, platform, baselines, real-robot evaluation, repeatability, limitations and source evidence. Being outside this batch's flagship-only scope does not establish low quality. Conversely, a flagship venue does not establish reliable transfer to WHEELTEC hardware. No new acquisition from those other venues was requested in this batch.

The baseline catalog contains 23 whole-paper assessments and 224 light cards. The other 116 active sources have no review card. Venue metadata alone cannot support a claim that every included method is strong or that every experiment was rechecked.
