# Local reading interface

The local `INDEX.html` is an offline Chinese reading interface, generated from admitted papers in the shared catalog (`main` and `surveys`). Excluded/background identities and pending acquisition records stay in the administrative CSV and do not inflate the displayed paper count. Styles, scripts and data are embedded; no server, CDN or network request is required to browse.

The page uses a compact catalog layout: a collection summary above the content, a left sidebar for research directions, years and sources, and a searchable paper table on the right. All summary and sidebar counts describe the full reading collection, independently of list filters. Annual ICRA/IROS/RSS/CoRL editions are grouped into conference families. The year chart shows the latest six bibliographic years with nonempty earlier years grouped into an earlier bin. Selecting a chart bar, source or research direction opens its matching papers with a fresh filter state. Source tabs separate journals and conferences; expanding the source list reveals every nonempty source. Venue counts describe holdings, not journal rankings.

The paper list supports inclusive start/end years, research direction, publication type and publication source. Filter options and their counts respect the other filters; zero-count alternatives are omitted. An applied filter that becomes empty remains selected without a zero-count option, and its removable chip permits recovery. Search matches all words across titles, identifiers, DOI, full and short venue names, research directions and scope notes. Results show only nonempty journal/conference counts. Sorting, pagination, result totals and CSV export use exactly the same matched set. CSV export includes all matches, not just the visible page, with Chinese column labels and UTF-8 BOM for spreadsheet use.

The interface uses plain-language labels, a restrained white/gray palette, blue text links and consistent sans-serif typography. Classification rows and thin dividers replace decorative cards, icons and oversized statistics. Paper titles, venue names and years are prominent; internal identifiers, source notes and derived-citation notices are in expandable paper details. PDF, full text, citation, publisher and reading-note links appear only when recorded and use a new tab. On mobile, the sidebar becomes a compact classification area and paper rows stack with thin separators. The URL fragment stores list filters and pagination for bookmarking; `/` focuses search outside form fields. Reduced-motion preferences and keyboard focus are supported. No journal quartile or implementation availability is inferred from venue names.

Run `python -m tools.build_indexes --strict` after catalog changes. The renderer is `tools/local_index.py`, its template is `tools/local_index.html`, and `tools/build_indexes.py` invokes it. The Chinese UI template is the only language exception in the public-tree checker; source-file, credential and absolute-path checks remain enforced. Public research prose and documentation stay English. Preserve JSON escaping and text-only rendering of catalog data. The generated local HTML stays excluded from Git; its generator and template are versioned.
