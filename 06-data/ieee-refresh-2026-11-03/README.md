# Recent IEEE refresh: registered and assessed sources

> **Stage record, November 3, 2026.** Its counts and statuses describe that date and are not updated. Current totals are in the [repository README](../../README.md).

**36 verified official PDF/native-BibTeX pairs, 36 OXR Markdown files and 36 targeted light review cards.** All 36 are manuscript members. At integration, the library had 231 acquired and assessed publications, and the manuscript cited 229 selected works.

The batch contains 23 formal-year 2026 and 13 formal-year 2025 papers: six each in localization, mapping, planning/control, collaboration, semantic navigation, and reliability/deployment. There are 34 journal articles and two ICRA papers. Exact DOIs were checked against the original 195-source library before freezing the selection. Formal issue and online dates can differ: R02 retains the publisher's 2026 issue year despite its 2024 DOI/online date. Raw IEEE BibTeX bytes were preserved.

The user supplied all 72 files after earlier automated navigation returned Request Rejected. The installed acquisition skill matched the files against the immutable selection and checked PDF identity, DOI, title, authors, venue and year. R13 represents epsilon as LaTeX in the selected title/BibTeX and a Unicode glyph in the PDF; a documented parsing-view normalization preserves both original files and the frozen selection. No publisher requests were needed for this import. See the [import receipt](manual-import.md).

The server ran SoMarkAI/OXR-1.0 through vLLM with OXR D-FINE layout analysis. All **482 physical PDF pages** were transcribed, with per-page JSON, page markers and **907 referenced image assets** archived in matching theme directories. There are 5 retained pipeline warnings; [coverage and quality checks](markdown-coverage.md) describe their resolution and limits. Both task-owned services were stopped after extraction, and their ports were verified free.

## Assessment

On November 3, 2026, each paper received a targeted reading of identity, abstract and selected method, setup, evaluation and limitation passages. The [reading ledger](source-reading.json) records the physical pages and retained excerpts; every excerpt matches the Markdown and the PDF text layer, and every card passes `tools.review_check --pdf`. Table values were not re-read from rendered pages, and the readings do not establish whole-paper verification. Numbers that the manuscript reports from these papers were located on their PDF pages. riMESA (R23) is a direct extension of iMESA (E40); both remain separate entries, and the cards state the relation. The [integration analysis](../../04-analysis/ieee-refresh-integration-2026-11-03.md) maps each paper to its survey section.
