# Survey manuscript

*Wheeled Mobile Robot Autonomy Under Degradation: A Survey of Methods and Evaluation* lives in [manuscript/](manuscript/README.md) in this repository: main paper, supplement, LaTeX sources, figure sources, generated tables, bibliography, numerical evidence records and build instructions. It is a working draft with author information and submission declarations still to be completed. The manuscript was a separate repository until November 4, 2026. That repository has since been deleted from GitHub, and its history is kept in a local backup bundle outside this repository's history.

The current main paper has 24 pages and 238 references, all from 2021-2026, which is the maximum length of IEEE T-IV surveys (18 pages plus 6 paid pages). Its eight-page supplement records selection and evaluation details and holds the five tables of representative implementations. All 229 selected works appear in substantive prose, including the 36 papers of the [IEEE refresh](../06-data/ieee-refresh-2026-11-03/README.md). The [current revision record](manuscript/revision/REVIEW-FIXES-1.md) describes the low-cost fixes from a simulated two-reviewer review. Earlier records describe the [contributions, numeric audit, clean build and review outcome](manuscript/revision/CONTRIBUTIONS-AUDIT-AND-REVIEW.md), the [research roadmap, claim audit and related surveys](manuscript/revision/ROADMAP-AUDIT-AND-SURVEYS.md), the [evidence map of Table IV and the restructured prose](manuscript/revision/EVIDENCE-MAP-AND-PROSE.md), the [table move, synthesis table and grayscale figures](manuscript/revision/PAGE-LIMIT-AND-FIGURE-STYLE.md) and the [SVG figures and condensed text](manuscript/revision/FIGURES-AND-CONDENSATION.md).
