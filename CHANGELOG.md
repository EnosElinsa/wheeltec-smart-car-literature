# Changelog

Notable changes to the library and the survey manuscript, newest first. Dates come from the batch and revision records. The Git history starts with the first public snapshot on November 3, 2026. Each entry links the record that holds the details, and its counts are those of that date.

## 2026-11-11

- Library: [11 DRL navigation papers with public author implementations](06-data/drl-open-source-2026-11-11/README.md), official native pairs and 90 OXR pages; 626 catalog records and 455 active reading sets. Existing assessments and survey selections are unchanged.

## 2026-11-05

- Repository: MIT license for the code and CC BY 4.0 for the cards, analyses, data and documentation, with the manuscript unlicensed before publication ([license table](README.md#license)). Adds `CITATION.cff`, the Checks workflow, this changelog, the contributing guide and the analysis index, and marks dated records with a stage-record banner.
- Manuscript round 6 ([REVIEW-FIXES-6](05-survey/manuscript/revision/REVIEW-FIXES-6.md)): Section III becomes a three-paragraph review method, and the selection table moves to supplement S1. The AI-use acknowledgment is reduced to three sentences, Fig. 2 is redrawn and the Fig. 1 shades are evenly spaced. The main paper stays at 24 pages.
- Manuscript round 5 ([REVIEW-FIXES-5](05-survey/manuscript/revision/REVIEW-FIXES-5.md)): answers items M1-M4 and m2 of a simulated re-review. A review recheck through the adjudication log kept the physical evidence level of 17 of 19 works, lowered 2 and found 4 more drivetrain-dependent effects.
- Manuscript round 4, second part ([REVIEW-FIXES-4](05-survey/manuscript/revision/REVIEW-FIXES-4.md)): reporting checklist, minor review items B10-B21, AI-use acknowledgment and audits.
- Background records: the Crossref cited-reference lists of fifteen older records were removed.

## 2026-11-04

- Evidence coding ([evidence-coding](06-data/evidence-coding/README.md)): codebook version 1.0, primary codes of all 245 selected works with source locators, and a language-model second coding of 40 sampled works with adjudication. Codebook version 1.1 clarifies two rules after the agreement step.
- Manuscript round 4, first part: the evidence codes enter the manuscript with a degradation-source by function matrix, an interface ranking, a rebuilt Table I and a drivetrain table. Positioning against automated-driving standards raises the reference count from 265 to 270.
- Library: 16 works from 2021-2024 that an internal review of an earlier draft named as missing (K01-K16) join the library and the survey selection, which gives 247 publications and 245 selected works ([batch guide](06-data/review-additions-2026-11-04/README.md)).
- Manuscript rounds 1-3 ([REVIEW-FIXES-1](05-survey/manuscript/revision/REVIEW-FIXES-1.md), [-2](05-survey/manuscript/revision/REVIEW-FIXES-2.md), [-3](05-survey/manuscript/revision/REVIEW-FIXES-3.md)): fixes from a simulated two-reviewer review, foundational pre-2021 sources cited as background references outside the corpus, condensation of Sections IV-VIII and integration of K01-K16 (246 to 265 references).
- Manuscript structure ([FIGURES-AND-CONDENSATION](05-survey/manuscript/revision/FIGURES-AND-CONDENSATION.md), [PAGE-LIMIT-AND-FIGURE-STYLE](05-survey/manuscript/revision/PAGE-LIMIT-AND-FIGURE-STYLE.md), [EVIDENCE-MAP-AND-PROSE](05-survey/manuscript/revision/EVIDENCE-MAP-AND-PROSE.md), [ROADMAP-AUDIT-AND-SURVEYS](05-survey/manuscript/revision/ROADMAP-AUDIT-AND-SURVEYS.md), [CONTRIBUTIONS-AUDIT-AND-REVIEW](05-survey/manuscript/revision/CONTRIBUTIONS-AUDIT-AND-REVIEW.md)): SVG figures, condensation from 36 to 26 pages, module tables moved to the supplement, grayscale figures, the evidence-map table, the research roadmap, the claim and numeric audits and a clean-checkout build. The manuscript reached the 24-page limit of IEEE T-IV surveys.
- Repository: the manuscript moved into this repository, which became the project's only repository. The former manuscript repository was deleted from GitHub, and its history is kept in a local backup bundle.
